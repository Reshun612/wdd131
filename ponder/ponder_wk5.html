Accessible design checklist

  • Use semantic headings in a logical order.
  • Keep text and background colors easy to distinguish.
  • Provide meaningful alternative text for informative images.
  • Make layouts responsive so content works on small and large screens.
  • Include a skip link and visible keyboard focus styles.
